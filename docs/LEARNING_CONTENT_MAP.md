# Learning content map (draft 0.1)

This is a course blueprint, not a claim of complete CEFR coverage. The shipped app currently contains only seed lessons. Each full unit needs authored lesson scripts, licensed/recorded audio, answer rationale, teacher review and assessment data.

| Level | Communicative outcomes | Core grammar / language | Typical tasks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Exchange greetings and personal details; handle familiar, predictable needs; understand short, slow, clearly articulated speech and very short familiar texts | be, subject pronouns, articles, plurals, basic questions, present simple, there is/are, can, basic prepositions | introduce yourself, fill a form, order a drink, ask/give simple directions, describe a routine |
| A2 | Handle routine exchanges and immediate needs; describe background, simple past events and plans | past simple, count/non-count, comparatives, going to, present continuous, modals/requests, common phrasal verbs | travel/booking, shopping, tell a short event, ask for help, write a brief message |
| B1 | Maintain familiar conversations, narrate experience, explain plans/reasons and understand the main points of clear standard speech/text | present perfect vs past, future forms, first conditional, passive basics, reported speech, linking devices | work update, email, experience story, problem-solving discussion, short presentation |
| B2 | Interact with fluency and spontaneity; follow complex arguments on familiar topics; produce clear detailed texts | advanced clauses, modal deduction, conditionals, passive/reporting, register, collocations and discourse markers | meeting, negotiation, structured opinion essay, presentation Q&A, evaluate sources |
| C1 | Use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic and professional purposes; understand implied meaning and adapt register | nuance, idiomatic/collocational language, complex syntax, cohesion, hedging, rhetorical choices | chair a discussion, synthesize sources, persuasive briefing, nuanced response, formal/informal register shift |

## Unit template
Each unit has explicit can-do outcomes, prerequisite/review links, a short input (audio/text/dialogue), vocabulary in context, form-meaning-use grammar notes, guided practice, a productive task, answer explanations, accessible transcript/caption, and a mastery check. Core activities rotate listening, reading, spoken interaction/production and written interaction/production. Turkish hints are available but fade as proficiency grows.

## A1 starter sequence
1. Hello & introductions — greetings, names, country; be / subject pronouns; short spoken exchange + profile card.
2. Numbers, spelling & personal details — alphabet, numbers, forms; listening for details + spelling/numbers.
3. People and things — family/classroom objects; a/an, plurals, possessives; describe a simple picture.
4. My day — times and routines; present simple, do/does; listen to and write a daily routine.
5. Home and neighborhood — rooms, places, there is/are, basic prepositions; read a simple local listing.
6. Food and café — menu words, count/non-count, would like; listen/order/role-play/write a short order.
7. Around town — places, imperatives, can/can't; follow and give short directions.
8. Free time — likes/dislikes, frequency adverbs; ask/answer about hobbies.
9. Yesterday — high-frequency past forms as a bridge; short supported personal story.
10. Review & practical mission — integrated mini tasks and a criterion-based A1 checkpoint.

## Item/content authoring rules
- Every exercise has a stable ID, CEFR level, target skill, objective, accepted answer(s), explanation in Turkish, distractor rationale, and source/rights record for media.
- Avoid single-choice-only tests as the sole evidence of proficiency.
- Randomize distractors only where answer key and accessibility remain valid.
- Validate every answer key in unit tests; audio has a transcript and alternative path.
- Use licensed/original content; do not copy commercial course scripts or proprietary test items.
